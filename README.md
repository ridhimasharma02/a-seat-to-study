# A Seat to Study

A design-thinking prototype that helps LPU students find a quiet study seat with a power socket in the Central Library (Block 37) before they leave the hostel.

INT335 · Design Thinking · Project P06 · Lovely Professional University

## What it does

- Shows a seat map for three floors, with filters for free seats, power sockets and the quiet zone
- Lets you reserve a seat. If you don't check in, the hold ends on its own.
- Lets you check in, see how much of your study session is left, and release the seat when you leave
- Includes a 3D-style floor preview, occupancy by floor, and a campus map to the library

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any web browser. There is nothing to install or build. The fonts and the map load from Google, so you need an internet connection for those.

## The research behind it

30 student interviews and 23 survey responses shaped our empathy map, our persona and our How Might We question:

> How might we help students find a free, quiet seat without walking around looking for one?

## Note

All seat data is simulated and stored only in your browser. No real seat is booked at LPU, and the floor layouts are illustrations, not official plans.
